# Party Mismatch and Other Predictors of New York Two-Voter Household Dissolution

The goal of this project is to produce a research article that will be published on publish.fun.
The title of the article will be Party Mismatch and Other Predictors of New York Two-Voter Household Dissolution.

- The article will present high-quality, rigorous analysis.
- The article will be approachable and easy-to-read.
- The article will situate the results from analysis conducted in this project within the context of previous related research.

## Quick Description

Do couples who mismatch in political party affiliation break up at higher rates than those who match? An AI agent will investigate with voter registration data snapshots.

## Definitions

Voter
: a person registered to vote in the state of New York. In the raw data files, each row represents a registered voter.

Two Voter Household
: a full mailing address for which there are exactly two registered voters in one file

Dissolution
: a dissolution occurred when a Two Voter Household from a previous observation appears does not appear in a later observation, and one of the Voters is still present.

## Research Questions

1. At what rate did Two Voter Households dissolve at each new observation?
2. What is the rate of dissolution for each combination of party registration at each new observation?
